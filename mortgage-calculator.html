What is a Mortgage?

A mortgage is a loan used to buy real estate, where the property itself serves as collateral. The borrower makes scheduled payments to the lender over a set term — typically 15, 20, or 30 years. Mortgages are the largest debt most people ever take on.

The monthly payment has four components, known as PITI: Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance.

Mortgage Payment Formula

Monthly Principal & Interest:

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where:

  • M = monthly P&I payment
  • P = loan amount (price − down payment)
  • r = monthly interest rate (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = total number of payments (years × 12)

Monthly PITI: M + (Tax ÷ 12) + (Insurance ÷ 12)

Worked Example

Example: $300,000 home, $60,000 down, 6.5% APR, 30 years, $3,000 tax, $1,200 insurance.

Loan amount: $300,000 − $60,000 = $240,000

r = 6.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.005417

n = 30 × 12 = 360

M = 240,000 × [0.005417 × (1.005417)^360] / [(1.005417)^360 − 1]

P&I ≈ $1,517.31/month

Tax: $3,000 ÷ 12 = $250/month

Insurance: $1,200 ÷ 12 = $100/month

Total PITI ≈ $1,867.31/month

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a mortgage payment calculated?

Monthly mortgage payment is calculated using the amortization formula: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is principal, r is monthly interest rate, and n is number of payments.

What is PITI?

PITI stands for Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance. These four components make up a typical monthly mortgage payment.

What is PMI?

PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) is usually required when your down payment is less than 20%. It protects the lender if you default. It typically costs 0.5-1% of the loan amount per year.

How much house can I afford?

A common rule is that your monthly mortgage payment should not exceed 28% of your gross monthly income. Lenders also look at your total debt-to-income ratio, aiming for under 36%.

What is amortization?

Amortization is the schedule of loan payments showing how much of each payment goes to principal vs. interest. In early years, most of each payment is interest. Later, most is principal.

Should I choose a 15 or 30 year mortgage?

15-year: higher monthly payment, much less total interest, faster equity. 30-year: lower monthly payment, more total interest, more cash flow flexibility. Choose based on your budget and goals.

What is a fixed vs adjustable-rate mortgage?

Fixed-rate: interest rate stays the same for the loan's life. Predictable. Adjustable-rate (ARM): rate changes after an initial fixed period, usually lower at first but can rise later. Fixed is safer for most buyers.

What are closing costs?

Closing costs are fees paid at the end of a home purchase — typically 2-5% of the loan amount. They include appraisal fees, title insurance, loan origination fees, and prepaid taxes and insurance.

Can I pay off my mortgage early?

Yes. Most mortgages have no prepayment penalty. Paying extra toward principal — even a small amount each month — can save tens of thousands in interest over the loan.
