What is a Mortgage?

A mortgage is a loan used to purchase real estate, where the property itself serves as collateral. The borrower makes scheduled payments to the lender over a set term (typically 15, 20, or 30 years).

Mortgage Formula

Monthly Payment: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where P = principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = total number of payments

Mortgage Tips

  • Down Payment: 20% down avoids PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance)
  • 15 vs 30 Years: 15-year loans have higher payments but much less total interest
  • Shop Around: Even 0.5% difference in rate can save thousands
  • Extra Payments: Paying extra toward principal reduces total interest
  • Credit Score: Higher credit = lower interest rate

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in a monthly mortgage payment?

Principal, interest, taxes, and insurance (PITI). This calculator shows principal + interest only.

How much house can I afford?

A common rule is that your mortgage payment should not exceed 28% of your gross monthly income.

What is amortization?

Amortization is the schedule of loan payments showing how much goes to principal vs. interest over time.

Should I choose a 15 or 30 year mortgage?

15-year: higher monthly payment, less total interest. 30-year: lower monthly payment, more total interest.
