What is a Mortgage?

A mortgage is a loan used to purchase a home or real estate. The borrower agrees to pay back the loan over a set period (usually 15 or 30 years) with interest. The property itself serves as collateral — if the borrower fails to repay, the lender can take possession of the home.

Mortgages are the largest financial commitment most people make in their lifetime. Understanding how monthly payments are calculated helps you make smarter decisions about home affordability.

Mortgage Payment Formula

M = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where:

  • M = Monthly payment
  • P = Loan amount (Home price − Down payment)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Total number of monthly payments (Years × 12)

Worked Example

A $400,000 home with a 20% down payment ($80,000) means a loan of $320,000 at 6.5% for 30 years:

  1. Loan amount: $400,000 − $80,000 = $320,000
  2. Monthly rate: 6.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.005417
  3. Total payments: 30 × 12 = 360
  4. Monthly payment: $2,022.62

Types of Mortgages

  • Fixed-Rate Mortgage: Interest rate stays the same for the life of the loan. Most common and predictable.
  • Adjustable-Rate Mortgage (ARM): Interest rate changes periodically based on market conditions.
  • FHA Loan: Government-backed loan with lower down payment requirements.
  • VA Loan: For veterans and active military — often no down payment required.
  • Jumbo Loan: For homes above the conforming loan limit.

Factors That Affect Your Mortgage Payment

  • Home Price: Higher price = higher loan = higher payment
  • Down Payment: Larger down payment = smaller loan
  • Interest Rate: Even 0.5% difference can add thousands over 30 years
  • Loan Term: Longer term = lower monthly payment but more total interest
  • Property Taxes & Insurance: Usually included in monthly payment (escrow)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a monthly mortgage payment calculated?

Monthly payment = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

What is a down payment?

A down payment is the upfront cash you pay when buying a home. Common down payments are 5%, 10%, or 20% of the home price.

What is the difference between a 15-year and 30-year mortgage?

A 15-year mortgage has higher monthly payments but lower total interest. A 30-year mortgage has lower monthly payments but higher total interest.

Does a larger down payment reduce my monthly payment?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the loan amount, which lowers both your monthly payment and total interest paid.
