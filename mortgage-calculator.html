About Mortgage Calculator

Yeh free mortgage calculator aapki monthly home loan payment calculate karta hai. Home price, down payment, interest rate, aur loan term ke hisaab se aapki monthly payment, total interest, aur total payment pata chalti hai.

How to Use

  • Home Price enter karein
  • Down Payment enter karein
  • Annual Interest Rate (%) enter karein
  • Loan Term (Years) enter karein
  • "Calculate Mortgage" button dabayein

Mortgage Formula

M = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Jahan: P = Loan Amount (Home Price - Down Payment), r = Monthly Interest Rate, n = Total Months
