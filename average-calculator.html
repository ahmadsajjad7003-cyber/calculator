About Average Calculator

Yeh free average calculator aapko numbers ke set ka mean (average), median, mode, range, sum, aur count calculate kar ke deta hai. Statistics students, data analysts, aur daily life mein kaam aata hai.

How to Use

  • Numbers ko comma (,), space, ya new line se separate kar ke enter karein
  • "Calculate" button dabayein
  • Saare statistical results foran show ho jayenge

Definitions

  • Mean: Sum ÷ Count (average)
  • Median: Middle value jab numbers sorted hon
  • Mode: Sab se zyada baar aane wala number
  • Range: Max − Min
