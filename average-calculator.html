What is an Average?

An average is a single value that represents the center or typical value of a set of numbers. The most common type of average is the mean, but there are others — median, mode, and range — each useful in different situations.

Averages are used everywhere: in grades, sports statistics, economic data, scientific research, and daily decision-making.

Statistical Formulas

Mean: Sum of all values ÷ Count

Median: Middle value when sorted (average of two middle values if even count)

Mode: Most frequently occurring value(s)

Range: Maximum − Minimum

Worked Example

Example: Numbers: 4, 8, 6, 5, 3, 8, 9

Mean: (4+8+6+5+3+8+9) ÷ 7 = 43 ÷ 7 ≈ 6.14

Sorted: 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 8, 9 → Median = 6

Mode: 8 (appears twice) → Mode = 8

Range: 9 − 3 = 6

Count: 7

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between mean and median?

Mean is the arithmetic average (sum ÷ count). Median is the middle value when data is sorted. Median is less affected by outliers.

What if there are multiple modes?

If two or more values appear equally often, the data is multimodal. This calculator shows all modes.

When should I use median instead of mean?

Use median when your data has extreme outliers (like income data), as it gives a better "typical" value.

What is range useful for?

Range shows the spread of data — how far apart the smallest and largest values are. A small range means data is clustered; a large range means it's spread out.

What is the difference between population and sample mean?

Population mean (μ) uses all data points in a group. Sample mean (x̄) uses a subset. The formula is the same, but sample mean is used to estimate the population mean.

Can the average be a decimal?

Yes. The mean is often a decimal. For example, the average of 1 and 2 is 1.5.

What if I enter negative numbers?

Negative numbers are supported and calculated correctly. Just make sure to separate them properly (e.g., "−5, 10, −3").

What is a weighted average?

A weighted average assigns different importance (weights) to values. For example, if a course grade is 40% homework and 60% exam, you compute a weighted average. This calculator computes a simple (unweighted) average.

How many numbers can I enter?

There's no hard limit, but performance is best with a few thousand numbers. For very large datasets, use statistical software.
