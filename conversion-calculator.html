What is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter is a tool that converts measurements from one unit to another. Whether you're cooking with metric and imperial recipes, doing science homework, comparing international product specifications, or planning a trip abroad, a unit converter saves time and prevents errors.

How to Use This Unit Converter

  1. Choose a category — Length, Weight, Temperature, Area, Volume, Speed, or Time.
  2. Enter the value you want to convert.
  3. Select the from and to units.
  4. Click Convert — the result appears instantly.

Supported Units

  • Length: mm, cm, m, km, inch, foot, yard, mile
  • Weight: mg, g, kg, ton, ounce, pound, stone
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
  • Area: sq m, sq km, sq cm, sq ft, sq yd, acre, hectare
  • Volume: ml, l, cubic m, gallon (US), quart, pint, cup
  • Speed: m/s, km/h, mph, knot, ft/s
  • Time: ms, s, min, hour, day, week, month, year

Quick Conversion Reference

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm
  • 1 foot = 30.48 cm
  • 1 mile = 1.609 km
  • 1 kg = 2.205 lb
  • 1 gallon (US) = 3.785 L
  • °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • K = °C + 273.15

Why Use Unit Conversions?

  • Travel: Convert currency, distance, and temperature when visiting other countries.
  • Cooking: Convert grams to ounces, cups to milliliters.
  • Science: Physics, chemistry, and biology use multiple unit systems.
  • Engineering: Convert measurements between SI and imperial systems.
  • Fitness: Convert kg to lbs, km to miles for running.
  • Shopping: Compare products from different countries.

Metric vs. Imperial System

  • Metric System: Used in most of the world. Based on powers of 10 (meter, gram, liter).
  • Imperial System: Used mainly in the United States, UK (partially), and a few other countries. Uses inch, foot, pound, gallon.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert units?

Select the category, enter the value, choose from and to units, and click Convert.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. For example, 25°C = 77°F.

How many cm are in an inch?

1 inch = 2.54 centimeters. So 10 inches = 25.4 cm.

What is the difference between mass and weight?

Mass is the amount of matter (kg, lb). Weight is the force of gravity on that mass (Newtons). In everyday use, they're often used interchangeably.
