Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Short Version: CalcMaster respects your privacy completely. All calculations run in your browser — no data is sent to our servers. We don't collect, store, or sell your personal information.

This Privacy Policy describes how CalcMaster ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use our website (the "Service"). By using CalcMaster, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because CalcMaster runs entirely in your browser, we do not collect:

  • Your calculator inputs (weights, heights, loan amounts, dates, etc.)
  • Your calculation results
  • Your name, email, or personal information
  • Your location or IP address (beyond standard server logs)
  • Your browsing history on our site

All calculations happen locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted to us.

2. Information We May Collect

Like most websites, we may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit:

  • Server Logs: Standard web server logs may include your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. These are used for security and analytics only.
  • Cookies: We may use essential cookies for basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies.
  • Analytics: We may use privacy-respecting analytics to count visits. No personal data is collected.

3. How We Use Information

The limited technical information we collect is used only for:

  • Ensuring the Service works correctly
  • Detecting and preventing abuse or attacks
  • Understanding general usage patterns (aggregate only)

4. Third-Party Services

CalcMaster is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical data as described in their own privacy policy. We do not control GitHub's data practices.

We do not use advertising networks, tracking pixels, or data brokers.

5. Children's Privacy

CalcMaster is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete it.

6. Data Security

Since we don't collect or store your data, there is no risk of your personal information being breached through our Service. Your calculations are safe because they never leave your device.

7. Your Rights

Because we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. You have full control over your data by simply not sharing it with us.

8. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For users in the European Union: We do not process personal data, so GDPR data subject rights are not applicable. If you have concerns, please contact us.

9. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

For California residents: We do not sell personal information. We do not collect personal information beyond standard server logs.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@calcmaster.example
