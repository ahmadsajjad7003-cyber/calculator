What is a Strong Password?

A strong password is one that's difficult for attackers or automated tools to guess or brute-force. Cybersecurity experts recommend passwords that are:

  • Long: At least 12-16 characters
  • Random: No dictionary words, names, or patterns
  • Mixed: Uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
  • Unique: Different for every account

Password Security Best Practices

  • Use a unique password for every account.
  • Use a password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password, Dashlane) to store passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
  • Never share your passwords via email, chat, or text.
  • Avoid personal info — birthdays, names, pets, addresses.
  • Change passwords immediately if a service you use is breached.
  • Don't reuse passwords across sites — one breach can compromise everything.

How Long to Crack a Password?

Approximate time for a modern computer to brute-force passwords (assuming a billion guesses per second):

  • 6 characters (lowercase only): Instant
  • 8 characters (mixed): A few hours
  • 10 characters (mixed): Several years
  • 12 characters (mixed): Thousands of years
  • 16 characters (mixed): Billions of years

Rule of thumb: Every extra character makes your password exponentially harder to crack.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should my password be?

At least 12 characters for most accounts. For sensitive accounts (banking, email), use 16+ characters.

Are these passwords safe to use?

Yes. Our passwords use cryptographically secure randomness, and everything runs in your browser — no data is sent to any server.

What makes a password strong?

Length (12+ characters), a mix of character types, no dictionary words, and uniqueness across accounts.

Should I use the same password for multiple accounts?

No. Use a unique password for every account. If one site is breached, all your accounts are at risk.

What is a password manager?

A password manager (like Bitwarden, 1Password, or Dashlane) securely stores all your passwords so you only need to remember one master password.
