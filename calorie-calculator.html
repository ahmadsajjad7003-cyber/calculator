What are Calories?

Calories are units of energy. Your body needs calories from food to function. When you eat more calories than you burn, your body stores the excess as fat. When you eat fewer calories than you burn, your body uses stored fat for energy.

Calorie Formula (Mifflin-St Jeor)

  • Men: BMR = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age + 5
  • Women: BMR = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age − 161
  • TDEE: BMR × Activity Multiplier
  • Weight Loss: TDEE − 500 cal/day (for 0.5 kg/week loss)

Activity Level Guide

  • Sedentary: Desk job, little or no exercise
  • Lightly Active: Light exercise 1-3 days/week
  • Moderately Active: Moderate exercise 3-5 days/week
  • Very Active: Hard exercise 6-7 days/week
  • Extra Active: Very hard exercise or athletic training

Healthy Weight Loss Tips

  • Safe rate: Lose 0.5–1 kg (1–2 lbs) per week
  • Protein: Eat plenty of protein to preserve muscle
  • Water: Drink 8+ glasses of water daily
  • Sleep: 7–9 hours of sleep supports weight loss
  • Exercise: Combine cardio with strength training
  • Tracking: Log your food to stay on target

Frequently Asked Questions

How many calories should I eat per day?

The average adult needs 1,800–2,500 calories per day, depending on various factors.

What is BMR?

BMR is the number of calories your body burns at rest to maintain basic functions.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

To lose about 0.5 kg (1 lb) per week, create a deficit of about 500 calories per day from your TDEE.

What is TDEE?

TDEE is the total calories you burn per day, including BMR plus physical activity.
