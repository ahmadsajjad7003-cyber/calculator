What is a Scientific Calculator?

A scientific calculator is an advanced calculator that goes beyond basic arithmetic (+, −, ×, ÷). It supports trigonometric functions (sin, cos, tan), logarithmic functions (log, ln), exponential functions, factorials, and more. It's essential for students, engineers, scientists, and anyone working with advanced math.

How to Use This Scientific Calculator

  1. Choose mode: DEG for degrees or RAD for radians.
  2. Enter numbers: Click the number buttons or use your keyboard.
  3. Add functions: Click sin, cos, log, ln, √, etc.
  4. Close parentheses: Don't forget to close every ( with ).
  5. Press = : Click the equals button or press Enter.

Supported Functions

  • Trigonometry: sin, cos, tan, sin⁻¹, cos⁻¹, tan⁻¹
  • Logarithms: log (base 10), ln (natural log)
  • Exponents: xʸ (power), √ (square root), e (Euler's number)
  • Constants: π (pi ≈ 3.14159), e (≈ 2.71828)
  • Other: n! (factorial), |x| (absolute value), % (percent)

Worked Examples

Example 1: sin(30°) in DEG mode = 0.5

Example 2: log(100) = 2

Example 3: 5! = 5×4×3×2×1 = 120

Example 4: √(144) = 12

DEG vs RAD Mode

  • DEG (Degrees): A full circle is 360°. Use for everyday geometry.
  • RAD (Radians): A full circle is 2π. Use for calculus and physics.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a scientific calculator?

An advanced calculator that supports trigonometric, logarithmic, exponential, and other mathematical functions.

What is the difference between DEG and RAD?

DEG measures angles in degrees (360° in a circle). RAD measures angles in radians (2π in a circle).

What is factorial (!)?

Factorial (n!) is the product of all positive integers up to n. Example: 5! = 120.
