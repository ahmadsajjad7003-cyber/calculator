What is a Scientific Calculator?

A scientific calculator is an advanced calculator capable of performing calculations beyond basic arithmetic — including trigonometry, logarithms, exponentials, and factorials. It's essential for students, engineers, and scientists.

How to Use This Scientific Calculator

  1. Click buttons to enter your expression
  2. Use sin, cos, tan for trigonometry (input in radians)
  3. Use log for base-10 logarithm, ln for natural log
  4. Use √ for square root, x^y for powers
  5. Use π for pi, e for Euler's number
  6. Click "=" to calculate the result

Supported Functions

Trigonometric: sin(x), cos(x), tan(x)

Logarithmic: log(x) — base 10, ln(x) — natural

Powers & Roots: √x, x^y

Constants: π ≈ 3.14159, e ≈ 2.71828

Factorial: n!

Common Uses

  • Students: Solving physics, chemistry, and math problems
  • Engineers: Complex calculations for design and analysis
  • Scientists: Data analysis and formula evaluation
  • Finance: Compound interest and exponential growth
  • Everyday: Advanced calculations not possible on basic calculators

Frequently Asked Questions

Are trigonometric functions in degrees or radians?

This calculator uses radians. To convert degrees to radians, multiply by π/180.

What is the difference between log and ln?

log is base-10 logarithm. ln is natural logarithm (base e).

What does factorial (!) mean?

n! = n × (n-1) × (n-2) × ... × 1. For example, 5! = 5×4×3×2×1 = 120.

Can I use the keyboard?

Yes — numbers and operators work with your keyboard. Press Enter for "=".
