What is a Date Calculator?

A date calculator helps you perform two common calculations: finding the difference between two dates, and adding or subtracting days, months, or years from a specific date. These calculations are useful for planning events, calculating deadlines, tracking project timelines, or counting down to special occasions.

How to Use This Date Calculator

Date Difference Mode:

  1. Enter a start date.
  2. Enter an end date.
  3. Click "Calculate Difference" to see total days, weeks, months, and years between them.

Add/Subtract Mode:

  1. Enter a base date.
  2. Choose Add or Subtract.
  3. Enter years, months, and/or days to add or subtract.
  4. Click "Calculate" to see the result.

Worked Examples

Date Difference: From January 1, 2025 to December 31, 2025

Total: 364 days (2025 is not a leap year)

Add Days: January 1, 2025 + 100 days

Result: April 11, 2025

Common Uses of Date Calculations

  • Project Planning: Calculate deadlines and milestones
  • Event Countdown: How many days until a wedding, birthday, or holiday
  • Legal & Contracts: Notice periods and contract end dates
  • Pregnancy Tracking: Due date calculation (40 weeks)
  • Loan & Investment Maturity: When a fixed deposit matures
  • Age Calculation: Exact age between two dates
  • Vacation Planning: How many days until your trip

Date Facts

  • Leap Year: Every 4 years (with some exceptions in century years).
  • Days per Month: 28-31 days depending on the month.
  • Weeks per Year: 52 weeks + 1 or 2 extra days.
  • 365.25 Days: The actual length of a solar year.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I calculate the number of days between two dates?

Enter the start and end dates in the Date Difference tab. You'll see exact days, weeks, months, and years between them.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes, the date calculator automatically handles leap years (February 29) for accurate results.

How do I add days to a date?

Switch to the Add/Subtract tab, select your base date, choose Add or Subtract, and enter the years, months, or days.

Can I calculate future or past dates?

Yes, the calculator works with any date — past, present, or future.
