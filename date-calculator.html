What is a Date Calculator?

A date calculator helps you compute the duration between two dates or determine a future or past date by adding or subtracting days. It's useful for project planning, event countdowns, contract terms, and scheduling.

Date Formulas

Days Between: End Date − Start Date

New Date: Start Date ± N days

Leap Year Rule: Divisible by 4 (and not by 100 unless also by 400)

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator include the end date?

No, it calculates the difference between dates (end date not counted). Add 1 if you need to include both endpoints.

What are business days?

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding weekends. Holidays are not excluded in this calculator.

Can I calculate a date in the past?

Yes — enter a negative number of days in the Add/Subtract mode to find past dates.

How are months calculated?

Months are approximate — the calculator uses average month length for display purposes. Exact dates use the calendar.

What is a leap year?

A leap year has 366 days instead of 365, with an extra day on February 29. Leap years occur every 4 years, except for years divisible by 100 unless also divisible by 400.

How many days are in each month?

January 31, February 28 (29 in leap years), March 31, April 30, May 31, June 30, July 31, August 31, September 30, October 31, November 30, December 31.

What is the difference between calendar days and business days?

Calendar days include every day of the week. Business days exclude weekends (and often holidays). A 10-business-day period typically spans 14 calendar days.

What is the Julian date?

The Julian date is the number of days since January 1, 4713 BC. Astronomers use it for tracking time continuously. This calculator uses the Gregorian calendar.

Does this work for dates before 1900?

Yes, for dates in the Gregorian calendar (adopted in 1582). For earlier dates, calendar systems differed across regions.

Is this date calculator free?

Yes, 100% free. All calculations run in your browser — no data is sent to any server.
