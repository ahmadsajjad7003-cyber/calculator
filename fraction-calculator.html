Fraction Formulas

Addition: a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd

Subtraction: a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd

Multiplication: a/b × c/d = ac / bd

Division: a/b ÷ c/d = ad / bc

FAQs

Simplified fraction kya hai?

Simplified fraction wo hai jismein numerator aur denominator ka koi common factor nahi hota. Jaise 4/8 = 1/2.

Mixed number kya hai?

Mixed number ek whole number aur ek proper fraction ka combination hai. Jaise 7/4 = 1 3/4.
