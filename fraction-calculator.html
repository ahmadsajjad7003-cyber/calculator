What is a Fraction?

A fraction represents a part of a whole. It consists of two numbers: a numerator (top) and a denominator (bottom). For example, 3/4 means 3 parts out of 4 equal parts. Fractions are essential in cooking, measurement, construction, and advanced mathematics.

Fraction Formulas

Addition: a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd

Subtraction: a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd

Multiplication: a/b × c/d = ac / bd

Division: a/b ÷ c/d = ad / bc

Worked Examples

Addition: 1/2 + 1/3

  1. (1×3 + 1×2) / (2×3) = (3 + 2) / 6 = 5/6
  2. Decimal: 0.8333

Multiplication: 3/4 × 2/5

  1. (3×2) / (4×5) = 6/20
  2. Simplified: 3/10 = 0.3

Mixed Number: 7/4

  1. 7 ÷ 4 = 1 remainder 3
  2. Mixed number: 1 3/4

Types of Fractions

  • Proper Fraction: Numerator smaller than denominator (e.g., 2/5).
  • Improper Fraction: Numerator larger than denominator (e.g., 7/4).
  • Mixed Number: Whole number + proper fraction (e.g., 1 3/4).
  • Equivalent Fractions: Different numbers, same value (e.g., 1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8).
  • Simplified Fraction: Numerator and denominator have no common factors.

How to Simplify Fractions

  1. Find the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of numerator and denominator.
  2. Divide both by the GCD.
  3. Example: 8/12 → GCD is 4 → 8÷4 / 12÷4 = 2/3.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add fractions with different denominators?

Find a common denominator first. The formula is (a×d + c×b) / (b×d), then simplify.

What is a simplified fraction?

A simplified fraction has no common factors between numerator and denominator except 1.

What is a mixed number?

A mixed number combines a whole number and a proper fraction. For example, 7/4 = 1 3/4.

How do I divide fractions?

To divide a/b ÷ c/d, multiply by the reciprocal: a/b × d/c = (a×d)/(b×c).
