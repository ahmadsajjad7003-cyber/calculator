Triangle Formulas

Perimeter: P = A + B + C

Area (Heron's Formula): s = (A+B+C)/2, Area = √(s(s−A)(s−B)(s−C))

Angle (Law of Cosines): cos(A) = (b² + c² − a²) / (2bc)

Triangle Types

  • Equilateral: Teeno sides equal
  • Isosceles: Do sides equal
  • Scalene: Teeno sides different
  • Right: Ek angle 90°
