What is a Triangle?

A triangle is a three-sided polygon with three vertices. It's the simplest polygon and one of the most important shapes in geometry, engineering, and design. The sum of its interior angles is always 180°.

Triangles can be classified by their sides (equilateral, isosceles, scalene) or by their angles (acute, right, obtuse).

Triangle Formulas

Perimeter: P = a + b + c

Heron's Formula (Area): A = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)]

Where s = semi-perimeter = (a + b + c) / 2

Law of Cosines (Angles): cos(A) = (b² + c² − a²) / 2bc

Worked Example

Example: Sides 3, 4, 5 (a classic right triangle).

Perimeter = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12

Semi-perimeter = 12 ÷ 2 = 6

Area = √[6(6−3)(6−4)(6−5)] = √[6 × 3 × 2 × 1] = √36 = 6

Angles: A = 36.87°, B = 53.13°, C = 90°

Triangle type: Scalene + Right

Triangle Inequality Theorem

For any triangle, the sum of any two sides must be greater than the third side:

  • a + b > c
  • b + c > a
  • a + c > b

If any of these fails, the three sides cannot form a triangle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Heron's formula?

Heron's formula calculates the area of a triangle using only the three side lengths: A = √[s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)], where s is the semi-perimeter.

How do I know if three sides form a triangle?

Check the triangle inequality: a + b > c, b + c > a, and a + c > b. If all three are true, a valid triangle exists.

What is the sum of angles in a triangle?

Always 180° in Euclidean geometry — regardless of the triangle's shape or size.

What is a right triangle?

A triangle with one 90° angle. It satisfies the Pythagorean theorem: a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse (the longest side).

How is the area of a triangle calculated?

With base and height: Area = ½ × base × height. With three sides: use Heron's formula. Both give the same result.

What is the Law of Cosines?

The Law of Cosines relates side lengths to angles: c² = a² + b² − 2ab·cos(C). It's used to find angles when all three sides are known.

Can a triangle have a 0° angle?

No. In a non-degenerate triangle, all angles are greater than 0° and less than 180°. A 0° angle would mean the sides are collinear — not a triangle.

What is an equilateral triangle?

A triangle with all three sides equal. All three angles are 60°. It's the most symmetric triangle.

What is the altitude of a triangle?

The altitude is a perpendicular line from a vertex to the opposite side. Area = ½ × base × height, where height is the altitude.
