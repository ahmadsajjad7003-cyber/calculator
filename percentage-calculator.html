What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word "percent" comes from the Latin "per centum," meaning "by the hundred." For example, 25% means 25 out of every 100, which is equivalent to the fraction 25/100 or the decimal 0.25.

Percentages are used everywhere — from shopping discounts and tax rates to interest rates, statistics, and scientific data.

Percentage Formulas

1. Percentage of a Number:

Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

2. What Percent is X of Y:

Percentage = (X ÷ Y) × 100

3. Percentage Increase/Decrease:

Change % = ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100

Worked Examples

Example 1: What is 20% of 150?

(20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 0.2 × 150 = 30

Example 2: 30 is what percent of 150?

(30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 0.2 × 100 = 20%

Example 3: Price went from $100 to $125. What is the increase?

((125 − 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = 25% increase

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 = (20 × 150) ÷ 100 = 30.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original from the new value, divide by the original, then multiply by 100. Formula: ((New − Original) ÷ Original) × 100.

What is the difference between percentage and percent?

They mean the same thing. 'Percent' is the symbol (%). 'Percentage' is the concept. Both refer to a portion out of 100.

How do I convert a percentage to a decimal?

Divide by 100. For example, 25% = 25 ÷ 100 = 0.25.

How do I convert a fraction to a percentage?

Divide the numerator by the denominator, then multiply by 100. For example, 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.

What is a percentage point?

A percentage point is the difference between two percentages. For example, going from 10% to 15% is a 5 percentage point increase, but a 50% relative increase.

How do I find the original price after a discount?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount%). For example, if the sale price is $80 after 20% off: 80 ÷ 0.8 = $100 original.

Can percentages be greater than 100?

Yes. A 150% increase means the final value is 2.5 times the original. Percentages above 100 are common in growth metrics and finance.

How do I calculate a tip percentage?

Multiply the bill by the tip percentage divided by 100. For a 15% tip on a $40 bill: (15 ÷ 100) × 40 = $6.
