What is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. The word "percent" comes from the Latin "per centum," meaning "by the hundred." Percentages are used everywhere — in finance, shopping, statistics, and everyday life.

How to Use This Percentage Calculator

Our calculator has three modes:

  1. X% of Y: Find a percentage of a given number. Example: What is 20% of 150?
  2. X is what % of Y: Find what percent one number is of another. Example: 25 is what percent of 50?
  3. % Increase/Decrease: Find the percentage change between two values. Example: From 100 to 150.

Percentage Formulas

1. X% of Y: Result = (X / 100) × Y

2. X is what % of Y: Result = (X / Y) × 100

3. Percentage Change: Result = [(New − Old) / Old] × 100

Worked Examples

Example 1: What is 20% of 150? → Result = (20 / 100) × 150 = 30

Example 2: 25 is what percent of 50? → Result = (25 / 50) × 100 = 50%

Example 3: Percentage change from 100 to 150: → Result = [(150 − 100) / 100] × 100 = 50% increase

Common Percentage Uses

  • Shopping Discounts: A 30% off sale means you pay 70% of the price.
  • Tips at Restaurants: A 15% tip on a $40 bill is $6.
  • Interest Rates: Banks charge or pay interest as a percentage.
  • Test Scores: Getting 45 out of 50 is 90%.
  • Statistics: Polls, surveys, and data are often shown as percentages.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage?

To find what percent X is of Y, use: (X / Y) × 100.

How do I find the percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 = 30.

How is percentage increase calculated?

Percentage increase = [(New Value − Old Value) / Old Value] × 100.
