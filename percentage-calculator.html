How to Calculate Percentages

A percentage is a number or ratio expressed as a fraction of 100. It is often denoted using the percent sign, "%". Our free Percentage Calculator helps you solve three of the most common percentage problems instantly.

Percentage Formulas

1. What is X% of Y?

Formula: (X / 100) * Y = Result

Example: What is 20% of 80? → (20 / 100) * 80 = 16

2. X is what percent of Y?

Formula: (X / Y) * 100 = Result%

Example: 15 is what percent of 60? → (15 / 60) * 100 = 25%

3. Percentage Increase or Decrease

Formula: ((Y - X) / X) * 100 = Result%

Example: Increase from 50 to 75 → ((75 - 50) / 50) * 100 = 50% Increase

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 10% discount?

Divide the original price by 10 (or multiply by 0.10). For example, a 10% discount on $50 is $5. The final price would be $45.

Can a percentage be more than 100%?

Yes! If a value increases beyond its original amount, the percentage will be greater than 100%. For example, if a $100 item increases to $150, that is a 150% of the original value (or a 50% increase).
