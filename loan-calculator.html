What is a Loan Calculator?

A loan calculator helps you estimate the monthly payment, total interest, and total cost of a loan. It's useful for personal loans, student loans, business loans, and any fixed-rate installment loan.

Loan Payment Formula

Monthly Payment: M = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1)

Where:

  • M = monthly payment
  • P = principal loan amount
  • r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = total number of payments (years × 12)

Worked Example

Loan: $20,000 at 7.5% for 5 years

  1. r = 7.5 / 12 / 100 = 0.00625
  2. n = 5 × 12 = 60
  3. M = 20000 × 0.00625 × (1.00625)^60 / ((1.00625)^60 − 1)
  4. M ≈ $400.76/month
  5. Total paid ≈ $24,045.60
  6. Total interest ≈ $4,045.60

Types of Loans

  • Personal Loan: Unsecured, fixed rate, 1–7 years.
  • Student Loan: For education, low interest, flexible repayment.
  • Auto Loan: Secured by vehicle, 3–7 years.
  • Mortgage: For homes, 15–30 years, lowest rates.
  • Business Loan: For companies, varies widely.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is monthly loan payment calculated?

Using the amortization formula: M = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1).

What is amortization?

Amortization is paying off a loan through regular installments. Early payments are mostly interest; later payments are mostly principal.

Does a longer loan term mean lower payments?

Yes, but you'll pay more total interest.
