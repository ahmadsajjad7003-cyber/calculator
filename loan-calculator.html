What is a Loan Calculator?

A loan calculator is a tool that computes your monthly payment (also called EMI — Equated Monthly Installment), the total interest you'll pay, and the total cost of the loan. Whether you're taking a personal loan, student loan, or business loan, this calculator helps you understand the true cost before signing any agreement.

How to Use This Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount — how much you need to borrow.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate — offered by your lender.
  3. Enter the loan term — in years.
  4. Click "Calculate EMI" to see your monthly payment and total cost.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where:

  • P = Principal loan amount
  • r = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Total number of monthly payments (Years × 12)

Worked Example

A $50,000 loan at 7.5% annual interest for 5 years:

  1. Monthly rate: 7.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.00625
  2. Total months: 5 × 12 = 60
  3. Monthly EMI: $1,002.11
  4. Total interest paid: $10,126.60

Types of Loans

  • Personal Loan: Unsecured loan for any personal use. Higher interest rates.
  • Student Loan: For education. Often lower interest rates and deferred payments.
  • Business Loan: For starting or expanding a business.
  • Home Equity Loan: Borrowing against the equity in your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It's the fixed amount you pay your lender every month.

How is loan interest calculated?

Interest is calculated on the outstanding loan balance. As you pay off the loan, the interest portion decreases.

Should I choose a longer or shorter loan term?

A shorter term means higher monthly payments but less total interest. A longer term means lower monthly payments but more total interest.
