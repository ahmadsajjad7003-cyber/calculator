What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It's calculated from your weight and height to categorize your weight range.

BMI Formula

Metric: BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]²

Imperial: BMI = 703 × weight (lb) / [height (in)]²

BMI Categories (WHO)

  • Underweight: Below 18.5
  • Normal Weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0 and above

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI may not be accurate for athletes, pregnant women, elderly people, or children.

What is a healthy BMI range?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy.
