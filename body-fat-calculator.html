What is Body Fat Percentage?

Body fat percentage is the proportion of your total body weight that comes from fat tissue. Unlike BMI, which only considers height and weight, body fat percentage gives a much more accurate picture of your health and fitness.

Your body needs some fat for essential functions — protecting organs, storing energy, and regulating hormones. But too much or too little body fat can cause health problems.

How to Use This Body Fat Calculator

  1. Select your gender (male or female).
  2. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  3. Enter your height in centimeters.
  4. Measure your neck — just below the larynx (Adam's apple).
  5. Measure your waist — at the narrowest point (above the navel).
  6. Females only: Measure hips at the widest point.
  7. Click "Calculate Body Fat" to see your percentage.

U.S. Navy Body Fat Formula

Men: Body Fat % = 495 / (1.0324 − 0.19077 × log₁₀(waist − neck) + 0.15456 × log₁₀(height)) − 450

Women: Body Fat % = 495 / (1.29579 − 0.35004 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 × log₁₀(height)) − 450

All measurements in centimeters.

Worked Example

A male with 38 cm neck, 85 cm waist, and 175 cm height:

  1. waist − neck = 85 − 38 = 47 cm
  2. log₁₀(47) = 1.6721
  3. log₁₀(175) = 2.2430
  4. Body Fat % = 495 / (1.0324 − 0.19077 × 1.6721 + 0.15456 × 2.2430) − 450
  5. = ~18% (Average category for men)

Body Fat Categories

  • Essential Fat: Men 2-5%, Women 10-13% (minimum for survival)
  • Athletes: Men 6-13%, Women 14-20%
  • Fitness: Men 14-17%, Women 21-24%
  • Average: Men 18-24%, Women 25-31%
  • Obese: Men 25%+, Women 32%+

How to Measure Accurately

  • Use a flexible measuring tape — not a rigid one.
  • Measure in the morning — before eating or drinking.
  • Keep tape parallel to the floor — not tilted.
  • Don't pull too tight — the tape should sit snugly but not compress the skin.
  • Measure 2-3 times — and take the average for consistency.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

For men, 14-24% is healthy. For women, 21-31% is healthy.

How accurate is the U.S. Navy body fat method?

The U.S. Navy method is accurate to within 3-4% of DEXA scan results.

Where do I measure my neck and waist?

Measure your neck just below the larynx. Measure your waist at the narrowest point.

Why is body fat percentage better than BMI?

BMI doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat. Body fat percentage measures actual fat.
