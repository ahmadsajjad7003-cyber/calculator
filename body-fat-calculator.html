What is Body Fat Percentage?

Body fat percentage is the proportion of your total body weight that comes from fat tissue. Unlike BMI, which only measures weight relative to height, body fat percentage tells you how much of your body is actually fat versus lean mass (muscle, bone, water, organs).

This makes it a much better indicator of fitness and health than BMI alone, because it distinguishes between fat and muscle.

U.S. Navy Body Fat Formula

Men:

Body Fat % = 86.010 × log₁₀(waist − neck) − 70.041 × log₁₀(height) + 36.76

Women:

Body Fat % = 163.205 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) − 97.684 × log₁₀(height) − 78.387

All measurements in centimeters.

How to Measure

  • Neck: Measure below the larynx, keeping the tape slightly angled downward.
  • Waist (men): Measure at the level of the navel.
  • Waist (women): Measure at the narrowest point of the torso.
  • Hip (women): Measure at the widest point of the hips.

Use a flexible measuring tape. Keep it snug but not compressing the skin. Measure twice to be sure.

Worked Example

Example: A man, 175 cm tall, waist 85 cm, neck 38 cm.

Body Fat % = 86.010 × log₁₀(85 − 38) − 70.041 × log₁₀(175) + 36.76

Why Body Fat Matters

  • Health: High body fat increases risk of heart disease, diabetes, and other conditions.
  • Fitness: Tracking body fat helps you see real progress, even when the scale doesn't move.
  • Performance: Athletes optimize body fat for speed, strength, and endurance.
  • Body Composition: Two people can weigh the same but have very different body fat levels.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

For men, 10-20% is considered healthy. For women, 18-28% is healthy. Athletes typically have lower percentages.

How accurate is the Navy method?

The U.S. Navy method is typically accurate within 3-4% of a DEXA scan when measurements are taken correctly.

How can I reduce body fat?

Reduce body fat by eating a slight calorie deficit, doing strength training to preserve muscle, and staying consistent with activity.

What is the difference between body fat and BMI?

BMI uses only height and weight. Body fat percentage uses measurements to estimate how much of your body is fat. Body fat is more accurate for assessing health.

Why do women need more body fat than men?

Women naturally have more essential fat for reproductive health. The minimum healthy body fat for women is around 10-13%, versus 2-5% for men.

Can I measure body fat at home accurately?

The Navy method with a measuring tape is the most practical home method. Smart scales and calipers are other options, but each has its own accuracy range.

How often should I measure my body fat?

Every 2-4 weeks is ideal. Daily fluctuations are mostly water and food, not fat.

Does age affect body fat percentage?

Yes. Body fat tends to increase with age, partly due to muscle loss. Healthy ranges shift slightly upward as you age.

What is visceral fat?

Visceral fat is fat stored around internal organs. It's more dangerous than subcutaneous fat (under the skin) and is linked to heart disease and diabetes.
