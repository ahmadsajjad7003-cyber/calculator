What is a Time Calculator?

A time calculator helps you add or subtract hours, minutes, and seconds, or calculate the total duration between two times. It's useful for tracking work hours, planning events, calculating flight times, and managing schedules.

How to Use This Time Calculator

Time Duration Mode:

  1. Enter a start time (hours, minutes, seconds).
  2. Enter an end time.
  3. Click "Calculate Duration" to see total time elapsed.

Add/Subtract Mode:

  1. Enter Time 1 and Time 2.
  2. Choose Add or Subtract.
  3. Click "Calculate" to see the result.

Worked Examples

Duration: From 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM

Result: 8 hours 30 minutes

Addition: 2h 30m + 1h 45m

Result: 4h 15m 0s

Time Units

  • 1 Minute: 60 seconds
  • 1 Hour: 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds
  • 1 Day: 24 hours = 1,440 minutes = 86,400 seconds
  • 1 Week: 7 days = 168 hours

Common Uses of Time Calculator

  • Travel: Plan flight, train, and road trip times

  • Sports: Track race and workout times

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I add time?

Switch to the Add/Subtract tab, enter two times, select Add, and click Calculate.

Does the calculator use 12-hour or 24-hour format?

Use 24-hour format. For example, 5:00 PM = 17:00.

How is time duration calculated?

The duration is calculated by subtracting start time from end time. If the end time is earlier, the calculator assumes it's the next day (adds 24 hours).

Can I calculate seconds?

Yes. The calculator supports hours, minutes, and seconds for accurate results.
