What is a Time Calculator?

A time calculator lets you add, subtract, multiply, or divide time durations. It's useful for timesheets, project tracking, workout planning, and scheduling. Instead of manually converting minutes and seconds, you enter values and get an accurate result instantly.

Time Conversion Formulas

1 hour = 60 minutes = 3600 seconds

1 minute = 60 seconds

Decimal Hours: hours + minutes/60 + seconds/3600

Worked Example

Example 1 (Add): 2h 45m 30s + 1h 20m 45s

Total seconds = (2×3600 + 45×60 + 30) + (1×3600 + 20×60 + 45)

= 9930 + 4845 = 14,775 seconds

= 4h 6m 15s

Example 2 (Decimal): 4h 6m 15s → 4 + 6/60 + 15/3600 = 4.1042 decimal hours

Frequently Asked Questions

Can minutes or seconds be greater than 59?

Input should be 0-59 for minutes and seconds. The result automatically carries over (e.g., 90 minutes = 1h 30m).

What is decimal hours?

Decimal hours express time as a decimal number. For example, 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 decimal hours. Commonly used in billing and payroll.

How do I calculate my weekly work hours?

Use Add Time mode repeatedly to sum each day's hours. The result shows total hours, minutes, and decimal hours.

Can I subtract a larger time from a smaller time?

Yes. The calculator supports negative results if you subtract a larger time from a smaller one.

How do I convert hours to minutes or seconds?

Multiply hours by 60 for minutes, by 3600 for seconds. Use the calculator's result display for quick conversions.

What is the difference between duration and time of day?

Duration is an amount of time (e.g., 2h 30m). Time of day is a specific point (e.g., 2:30 PM). This calculator works with duration.

How is decimal time used in payroll?

Payroll systems often use decimal hours instead of hours and minutes. For example, 7 hours 30 minutes = 7.5 decimal hours. Multiply by the hourly rate to get pay.

Can I use this for cooking or baking?

Yes. Add times for multi-step recipes or calculate cooking durations in hours, minutes, and seconds.

What is 100 minutes in hours and minutes?

100 minutes = 1 hour 40 minutes (since 100 − 60 = 40). Enter 100 in the minutes field to see the result.

Is this time calculator free?

Yes, 100% free. All calculations run in your browser — no data is sent to any server.
