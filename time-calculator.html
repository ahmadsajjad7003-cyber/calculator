What is a Time Calculator?

A time calculator lets you add, subtract, multiply, or divide time durations. It's useful for timesheets, project tracking, workout planning, and scheduling.

How to Use This Time Calculator

  1. Select the operation: Add, Subtract, Multiply, or Divide
  2. Enter the time values (hours, minutes, seconds)
  3. Click "Calculate"
  4. See results in multiple formats

Time Conversion Formulas

1 hour = 60 minutes = 3600 seconds

1 minute = 60 seconds

Decimal Hours: hours + minutes/60 + seconds/3600

Common Uses

  • Timesheets: Add up work hours for the week
  • Project Management: Calculate total task duration
  • Fitness: Sum workout times across sessions
  • Music/Audio: Add track lengths to determine album duration
  • Travel: Calculate total travel time across legs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can minutes or seconds be greater than 59?

Input should be 0-59 for minutes and seconds. The result automatically carries over (e.g., 90 minutes = 1h 30m).

What is decimal hours?

Decimal hours express time as a decimal number. For example, 1 hour 30 minutes = 1.5 decimal hours. Commonly used in billing and payroll.

How do I calculate my weekly work hours?

Use "Add Time" mode repeatedly to sum each day's hours. The result shows total hours, minutes, and decimal hours.

Can I subtract a larger time from a smaller time?

The calculator supports negative results if you subtract a larger time from a smaller one.
