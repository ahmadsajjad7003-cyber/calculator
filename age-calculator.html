What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator is a tool that calculates your exact age based on your date of birth. Unlike just knowing your age in years, an age calculator gives you a precise breakdown in years, months, days, hours, and even minutes.

How to Use This Age Calculator

  1. Select your birth day from the first dropdown.
  2. Select your birth month from the second dropdown.
  3. Select your birth year from the third dropdown.
  4. Click "Calculate Age" to see your exact age.

Age Calculation Formula

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

The calculator accounts for:

  • Actual month lengths (28, 29, 30, or 31 days)
  • Leap years (February 29)
  • Time zones (uses your local time)

Worked Example

If someone was born on January 15, 1990 and today is June 20, 2025:

  1. Years: 2025 − 1990 = 35 years
  2. Months: June − January = 5 months
  3. Days: 20 − 15 = 5 days

So the person is 35 years, 5 months, and 5 days old.

Common Uses of Age Calculator

  • Job Applications: Many employers require exact age.
  • Government Forms: Passport, driving license, and visa applications.
  • Medical Records: Doctors use exact age to assess growth and health.
  • School Admissions: Age cutoff dates matter for grade placement.
  • Retirement Planning: Knowing exact age helps plan retirement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated?

Age is calculated by subtracting your date of birth from the current date, accounting for actual month lengths and leap years.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes, it automatically accounts for leap years.

Can I calculate age for a future date?

No, the date of birth cannot be in the future.
