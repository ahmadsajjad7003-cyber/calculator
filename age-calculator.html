About Age Calculator

Yeh free age calculator aapki exact age calculate karta hai aapki date of birth se. Yeh years, months, days, aur total hours/minutes mein result deta hai.

How to Use

  • Apni date of birth select karein
  • "Calculate Age" button dabayein
  • Aapki exact age foran dikhai degi

Formula

Age = Current Date - Date of Birth. Hum har month ki actual days (28/30/31) ko consider karte hain accurate result ke liye.
