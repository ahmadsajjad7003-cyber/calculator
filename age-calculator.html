What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines how old you are based on your date of birth and today's date (or any other date you choose). It gives you your exact age in years, months, and days — and can also show total days, hours, and minutes you have lived.

This is useful for filling out forms, checking eligibility for events or benefits, planning birthdays, or simply satisfying curiosity about exactly how long you have been alive.

How Age is Calculated

Basic Formula:

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

Step-by-step:

  1. Subtract the birth year from the current year
  2. Subtract birth month from current month
  3. Subtract birth day from current day
  4. If days are negative, borrow from the month
  5. If months are negative, borrow from the year

This method accounts for different month lengths (28, 30, 31 days) and leap years.

Worked Example

Example: Born on 15 March 1995. Today is 20 October 2026.

Years: 2026 − 1995 = 31

Months: 10 − 3 = 7

Days: 20 − 15 = 5

Age = 31 years, 7 months, 5 days

Why Leap Years Matter

A leap year has 366 days instead of 365, with an extra day on February 29. Leap years occur every 4 years, except for years divisible by 100 unless also divisible by 400. Over a lifetime, these extra days add up, which is why precise age calculators account for them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is age calculated?

Age is calculated by finding the difference between your date of birth and today's date. The result is expressed in years, months, and days, accounting for leap years and varying month lengths.

Why do leap years affect age calculation?

Leap years add an extra day (February 29) every four years. This extra day slightly changes the total number of days you have lived, which is why accurate age calculators account for it.

Can I calculate my age in hours or minutes?

Yes. Once your total days are known, multiply by 24 for hours, or by 1440 for minutes. Our calculator does this automatically.

Does my age change on my birthday or the day before?

Your age increases on your birthday. On the day before, you are still the previous age. Some cultures count age differently, but this calculator uses the standard international method.

What if I was born on February 29?

People born on February 29 (leap day) usually celebrate their birthday on February 28 or March 1 in non-leap years. For legal purposes, many countries treat March 1 as the birthday in non-leap years.
