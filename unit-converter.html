What is Unit Conversion?

Unit conversion is the process of changing a measurement from one unit to another. Different countries and industries use different measurement systems, so conversion is essential for science, engineering, cooking, travel, and everyday life.

The two main systems are the metric system (meters, kilograms, liters) used in most countries, and the imperial system (feet, pounds, gallons) used mainly in the United States.

Temperature Conversions

Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Celsius to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15

Worked Example

Example 1: Convert 5 miles to kilometers.

5 × 1.60934 = 8.047 km

Example 2: Convert 25°C to Fahrenheit.

(25 × 9/5) + 32 = 45 + 32 = 77°F

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Use the formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. For example, 25°C = (25 × 9/5) + 32 = 77°F.

How many feet are in a meter?

1 meter = 3.28084 feet. To convert meters to feet, multiply by 3.28084.

How many grams are in an ounce?

1 ounce = 28.3495 grams. To convert ounces to grams, multiply by 28.3495.

What is the difference between mass and weight?

Mass is the amount of matter in an object (kg). Weight is the force of gravity on that mass (Newtons). In everyday use, we often say "weight" when we mean mass.

How do I convert miles to kilometers?

Multiply miles by 1.60934. For example, 10 miles = 16.0934 km.

How many liters are in a gallon?

1 US gallon = 3.78541 liters. 1 UK (imperial) gallon = 4.54609 liters. This converter uses US gallons.

How do I convert kg to pounds?

Multiply kilograms by 2.20462. For example, 70 kg = 154.32 pounds.

What are significant figures?

Significant figures indicate the precision of a measurement. This converter displays up to 6 significant figures.

Can I convert between any unit?

This converter handles common metric and imperial units. For highly specialized units, use a dedicated scientific tool.
