About the BMI Calculator

The Body Mass Index (BMI) Calculator is a free online tool used to calculate your BMI value and corresponding weight status while taking age and gender into consideration. Use the "Metric Units" tab for the International System of Units (kg/cm) or the "US Units" tab to convert units into pounds and feet/inches.

BMI Formula

Below are the equations used for calculating BMI in both Metric and US Customary systems:

  • Metric Units: BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]²
  • US Units: BMI = 703 × weight (lbs) / [height (in)]²

Risks Associated with Being Overweight

According to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), being overweight increases the risk of several serious health conditions, including:

  • High blood pressure and higher LDL ("bad") cholesterol
  • Type II diabetes and coronary heart disease
  • Stroke, gallbladder disease, and osteoarthritis
  • Sleep apnea, breathing problems, and certain cancers

Limitations of BMI

While BMI is a widely used and useful indicator of healthy body weight, it does have limitations. It cannot take body composition (muscle vs. fat) into account. For example, highly trained athletes or bodybuilders may have a high BMI due to large muscle mass, but they are not necessarily overweight or unhealthy. Always consult a healthcare professional for a comprehensive health assessment.
