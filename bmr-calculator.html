What is BMR?

BMR stands for Basal Metabolic Rate. It's the number of calories your body needs to perform basic life-sustaining functions while at complete rest. These functions include breathing, circulating blood, controlling body temperature, cell growth, and brain and nerve function.

BMR typically accounts for 60-70% of your total daily calorie expenditure. Even if you lay in bed all day without moving, your body would still burn your BMR in calories.

BMR Formula (Mifflin-St Jeor)

Men: BMR = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age + 5

Women: BMR = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age − 161

TDEE: BMR × Activity Multiplier

Weight: kilograms | Height: centimeters | Age: years

Worked Example

A 30-year-old male, 70 kg, 175 cm:

  1. BMR = 10(70) + 6.25(175) − 5(30) + 5
  2. BMR = 700 + 1093.75 − 150 + 5
  3. BMR = 1,648.75 cal/day

If moderately active (×1.55), TDEE = 1,648.75 × 1.55 = 2,555 cal/day.

BMR vs TDEE

  • BMR: Calories burned at complete rest (60-70% of daily total).
  • TEF: Thermic Effect of Food — calories burned digesting food (10%).
  • EAT: Exercise Activity Thermogenesis — calories burned during exercise (5-10%).
  • NEAT: Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis — calories burned from daily movement (15-20%).
  • TDEE: Total Daily Energy Expenditure = BMR + TEF + EAT + NEAT.

Factors That Affect BMR

  • Age: BMR decreases by ~2% per decade after age 20.
  • Gender: Men have higher BMR due to greater muscle mass.
  • Muscle Mass: Muscle burns more calories than fat at rest.
  • Weight: Heavier people have higher BMR.
  • Genetics: Some people naturally have higher metabolism.
  • Hormones: Thyroid problems can affect BMR significantly.

How to Increase Your BMR

  • Build muscle: Strength training 3-4 times per week.
  • Eat protein: Protein has a high thermic effect.
  • Sleep well: 7-9 hours of quality sleep supports metabolism.
  • Stay active: Take the stairs, walk more, fidget.
  • Avoid crash diets: Extreme calorie restriction lowers BMR.
  • Drink water: Staying hydrated supports metabolism.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is BMR?

BMR is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest to maintain basic functions like breathing and heartbeat.

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is calories burned at rest. TDEE is BMR plus all calories burned through daily activities and exercise.

What is a normal BMR?

For most adults, BMR ranges from 1,200 to 2,000 calories per day. Men typically have higher BMR than women.

How can I increase my BMR?

Build muscle through strength training, eat enough protein, get adequate sleep, and avoid extreme calorie restriction.
