What is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It's a numerical value calculated from a person's weight and height. BMI is widely used as a screening tool to categorize adults into weight ranges: underweight, normal, overweight, and obese.

BMI Formula

Metric: BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]²

Imperial: BMI = 703 × weight (lb) / [height (in)]²

BMI Categories (WHO)

Frequently Asked Questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI may not be accurate for athletes, pregnant women, elderly people, or children.

What is a healthy BMI range?

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered healthy.
