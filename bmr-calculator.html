About BMR Calculator

Yeh free BMR calculator aapki Basal Metabolic Rate calculate karta hai — yani wo calories jo aapka body aaram ki halat mein bhi burn karta hai (sirf saans lene, heart beat, body temperature maintain karne ke liye).

How to Use

  • Age aur Gender enter karein
  • Weight (kg) aur Height (cm) enter karein
  • Activity Level select karein
  • "Calculate BMR" button dabayein

BMR Formula (Mifflin-St Jeor)

Men: BMR = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height - 5 × age + 5

Women: BMR = 10 × weight + 6.25 × height - 5 × age - 161

TDEE: BMR × Activity Multiplier

FAQs

BMR aur TDEE mein farq kya hai?

BMR sirf resting calories hain. TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) aapki daily activity ke saath total burn calories hain.

BMR kam kaise karein?

BMR naturally age ke saath kam hota hai. Muscle mass barhane se BMR barhta hai. Extreme dieting se BMR kam ho jata hai.
