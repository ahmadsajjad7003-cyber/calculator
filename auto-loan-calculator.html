What is an Auto Loan?

An auto loan is a secured loan used to purchase a vehicle. The vehicle serves as collateral, and the borrower makes monthly payments over a set term — typically 36, 48, 60, or 72 months.

How to Use This Auto Loan Calculator

  5. Enter the loan term in months

Auto Loan Formula

Monthly Payment: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where P = loan amount (price − down payment − trade-in), r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months

Auto Loan Tips

  • Larger Down Payment: Reduces loan amount and monthly payment
  • Shorter Term: Higher monthly payment but less total interest
  • Shop for Rates: Credit unions often offer lower rates than dealers
  • Credit Score: Aim for 700+ for the best rates
  • 20/4/10 Rule: 20% down, 4-year loan, 10% of income for car expenses

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good interest rate for an auto loan?

It depends on your credit score. Excellent credit (720+) can get rates as low as 3-5%. Poor credit may see 10-20%.

Should I finance through the dealer or my bank?

Always compare both. Get pre-approved by your bank or credit union first, then negotiate.

How does trade-in value affect my loan?

Trade-in value reduces the amount you need to finance, lowering both monthly payment and total interest.

Is a longer loan term better?

No — longer terms mean lower monthly payments but much more total interest paid.
