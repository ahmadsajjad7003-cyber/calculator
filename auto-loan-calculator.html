About Auto Loan Calculator

Yeh free auto loan calculator aapki monthly car payment calculate karta hai. Vehicle price, down payment, trade-in value, interest rate, aur loan term ke hisaab se aapki monthly payment, total interest, aur total cost pata chalti hai.

How to Use

  • Vehicle Price enter karein
  • Down Payment enter karein
  • Trade-in Value enter karein (agar koi purani car exchange kar rahe hain)
  • Annual Interest Rate (%) enter karein
  • Loan Term (Months) enter karein
  • "Calculate Auto Loan" button dabayein

Auto Loan Formula

M = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n - 1]

Jahan: P = Loan Amount (Vehicle Price - Down Payment - Trade-in), r = Monthly Interest Rate, n = Total Months

FAQs

Trade-in value kya hai?

Trade-in value wo raqam hai jo dealership aapki purani car ke badle deta hai. Yeh nayi car ki price se minus ho jati hai.
