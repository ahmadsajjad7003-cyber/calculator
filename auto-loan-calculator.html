What is an Auto Loan?

An auto loan is a secured loan used to purchase a vehicle. The car itself serves as collateral — meaning if you fail to repay, the lender can repossess it. Auto loans typically have terms of 36, 48, 60, or 72 months, with the most common being 60 months (5 years).

The loan amount is the vehicle price minus your down payment and any trade-in value, plus taxes and fees.

Auto Loan Payment Formula

Monthly Payment:

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where:

  • M = monthly payment
  • P = loan amount (price − down payment − trade-in + tax)
  • r = monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = loan term in months

Worked Example

Example: $30,000 car, $5,000 down, $3,000 trade-in, 7% tax, 6.5% APR, 60 months.

Taxable amount: $30,000 − $3,000 = $27,000

Sales tax: 7% × $27,000 = $1,890

Loan amount: $30,000 − $5,000 − $3,000 + $1,890 = $23,890

r = 6.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.005417

M = 23,890 × [0.005417 × (1.005417)^60] / [(1.005417)^60 − 1]

M ≈ $467.40/month

Total payment: $467.40 × 60 = $28,044

Total interest: $28,044 − $23,890 = $4,154

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a car payment calculated?

Monthly car payment is calculated using the amortization formula: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is loan amount, r is monthly interest rate, and n is number of months.

What is a good interest rate for a car loan?

It depends on your credit score. Excellent credit (720+) can get 3-5%. Good credit (660-719) gets 5-8%. Fair credit gets 8-15%. Poor credit may see 15%+.

Should I choose a longer loan term?

Longer terms lower monthly payments but increase total interest and risk being upside-down on the loan. 48-60 months is generally ideal.

What is the 20/4/10 rule?

A common car-buying guideline: 20% down payment, 4-year loan term, and total car expenses (payment + insurance + gas) no more than 10% of gross monthly income.

What does "upside-down" mean?

Being upside-down (or underwater) means you owe more on the car than it's worth. This happens with small down payments and long loan terms.

Should I get pre-approved before shopping?

Yes. Pre-approval from a bank or credit union gives you a rate to compare against dealer financing and strengthens your negotiating position.

Is dealer financing or bank financing better?

Dealers often offer promotional rates (0% APR) on new cars. Banks and credit unions often have lower rates on used cars. Always compare both.

Does a larger down payment help?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the loan amount, lowers monthly payments, reduces total interest, and helps you avoid being upside-down.

Can I pay off my car loan early?

Most auto loans have no prepayment penalty. Paying early saves interest. Check your loan agreement first.
