What is an Auto Loan?

An auto loan is a type of secured loan used to purchase a vehicle. The car itself acts as collateral — if you don't repay the loan, the lender can repossess the vehicle. Auto loans typically have lower interest rates than personal loans because they're secured.

Most auto loans are repaid over 36, 48, 60, or 72 months. Shorter terms have higher monthly payments but lower total interest.

How to Use This Auto Loan Calculator

Auto Loan Formula

M = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where:

  • M = Monthly payment
  • P = Loan amount (Vehicle price − Down payment − Trade-in)
  • r = Monthly interest rate (Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  • n = Total number of monthly payments

Worked Example

$30,000 car, $5,000 down payment, 6.5% interest, 60-month term:

  1. Loan amount: $30,000 − $5,000 = $25,000
  2. Monthly rate: 6.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.005417
  3. Monthly payment: $489.12
  4. Total interest: $4,347.20

How to Get the Best Auto Loan

  • Check your credit score — higher scores get lower rates.
  • Get pre-approved — from your bank or credit union before visiting the dealer.
  • Compare rates — from at least 3 lenders.
  • Consider a shorter term — saves on total interest.
  • Make a larger down payment — reduces loan amount and monthly payment.
  • Avoid long terms — 72 or 84-month loans often lead to being "upside down."

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a car payment calculated?

Monthly car payment = P × r × (1+r)^n / [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

What is trade-in value?

Trade-in value is the amount a dealership offers for your current vehicle when you buy a new one.

Is a longer auto loan term better?

A longer term means lower monthly payments but higher total interest.

What is a good interest rate for a car loan?

New car loans typically range from 3% to 10%, and used car loans from 5% to 15%.
