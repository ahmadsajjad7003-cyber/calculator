Interest Formulas

Simple Interest: SI = P × R × T / 100

Compound Interest: A = P × (1 + r/n)^(nt)

Jahan: P = Principal, R = Annual Rate, T = Time (years), r = Rate/100, n = Compounding frequency (monthly = 12)

FAQs

Simple aur Compound interest mein farq kya hai?

Simple interest sirf principal par milta hai. Compound interest principal + pichla interest dono par milta hai, isliye yeh zyada return deta hai.
